Frequently Asked Questions

Everything families ask us before they start

How quickly can care begin?
In most cases we can match a caregiver and begin care within 48 hours of your free in-home assessment. For urgent situations — like a hospital discharge — we do our best to accommodate same-day or next-day starts. Call (407) 818-5647 and tell us it's urgent.
Are your caregivers background-checked?
Yes — every caregiver completes a Level 2 background screening (fingerprint-based, state and federal), reference verification, and a skills assessment before they ever enter a client's home. No exceptions.
Do you really guarantee a Spanish-speaking caregiver?
Yes. Our community is more than 60% Hispanic, and most of our caregivers are bilingual. If your family requests a Spanish-speaking caregiver, that's who you get — for every visit, not just the first one.
How much does home care cost?
It depends on the hours and level of care your loved one needs — which is exactly what the free in-home assessment determines. You'll get a clear, written care plan with exact pricing before you commit to anything, and there are no long-term contracts or hidden fees.
What if we don't like the caregiver?
Tell us — no awkward conversation needed on your end. Under our Right-Match Guarantee we re-match your loved one with a new caregiver at no extra charge, quickly. The right relationship is the whole point of our matching process.
Do you offer care on weekends and holidays?
Yes. Care doesn't take days off, and neither do we. We schedule weekends, holidays, overnights, and 24-hour rotations based on your family's needs.
Can you help right after a hospital discharge?
Yes — post-hospital recovery is one of our most requested services. We can coordinate before discharge so a caregiver is ready the day your loved one comes home, when fall and readmission risk is highest.
Do we have to sign a long-term contract?
No. You're never locked in. Adjust hours up or down, pause, or stop as your family's situation changes. Families stay with us because the care is good — that's the only contract we want.
What areas do you serve?
Poinciana (our headquarters), Kissimmee, St. Cloud, Davenport, Haines City, and South Orlando neighborhoods like Hunters Creek and Meadow Woods. Nearby? Call us — if we can serve you, we will.
What happens after I submit the form?
A care coordinator (English or Spanish — your choice) calls you back, usually the same day. We listen to your situation, schedule the free in-home assessment, and you decide from there. No pressure, no obligation.
